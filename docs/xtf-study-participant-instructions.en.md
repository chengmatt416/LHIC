# Participant instructions — LHIC LearnLoop offline intent study

Version: `[INSTRUCTION_VERSION]`

These instructions are for approved, consented participants. They do not replace the participant information and consent form.

## Before you begin

- Use only the study device, browser profile, and web pages provided by the study operator.
- Do not sign in to a real personal account.
- Do not enter a real password, private message, payment detail, medical detail, school identifier, government identifier, or other sensitive information.
- Use only the synthetic values shown by the study operator.
- Ask a question before continuing when a task is unclear.
- You may pause, skip a task, or stop at any time.

## During each task

1. Read the task shown by the study operator.
2. Confirm that the page is a study fixture and does not require real personal data.
3. Complete only the stated task. Do not explore unrelated controls.
4. Do not open another tab, another account, developer tools, source code, or private logs unless the approved task explicitly instructs you to do so.
5. Tell the operator when you believe the task is complete.
6. Do not try to improve, correct, or explain the model prediction. The study measures the software under a fixed protocol.

## Stop immediately when

- the page asks for real credentials or sensitive information;
- the page appears to affect a real service, account, payment, booking, message, or public post;
- unexpected content appears;
- you feel uncomfortable, confused, or pressured;
- the operator asks you to do something different from the approved instructions.

Stopping will not cause a penalty.

## What the software records

The approved dataset records pseudonymous hashes and bounded decision/timing measurements. It must not record your name, contact details, raw task text, raw page text, screenshot, URL, selector, password, private message, or free-form explanation.

Tell the operator immediately if you believe any prohibited information was captured.

## Withdrawal

You may request withdrawal using the contact and method listed in the consent form. The project team will use a separate restricted mapping to identify your study code. The withdrawal process has the limits described in the consent form, including the boundary for already published aggregate results.

## After the session

Do not share task screenshots, restricted schedules, participant codes, or study files. You may discuss your general experience unless the responsible supervisor has stated a specific confidentiality rule.
